# slimstack: chibi illustration brief (for Gemini)

Paste the **Final prompt** block into Gemini. The sections above it are the reasoning and the editable parts.

## What slimstack is (one line)

A token-lean workflow plugin for AI coding agents: 12 skills that take an idea from brainstorm to merged code, a Bash output compactor that squeezes huge logs, and a local Kanban board where **you are the orchestrator** and headless agents code, test, and reply when you act.

## Concept

A cozy chibi dev-team workshop. A slim stack of glowing tokens is the mascot. A tiny human orchestrator stands at the center of a Kanban board and directs a crew of chibi agents. Everything is small, round, and busy, but tidy: the plugin's promise is "lean".

## Characters (all chibi: 2-3 heads tall, huge heads, tiny bodies, big sparkly eyes)

| Role | Maps to | Look and prop |
|---|---|---|
| **Slim** (mascot) | slimstack / token-lean | A tall, slim stack of 5 round golden token coins with a sleepy-happy face and tiny arms. Sits on the board's top edge. |
| **Orchestrator** | You, the user | Chibi human in a hoodie and conductor's armband, holding a baton-shaped pointer. Standing center, pointing at a ticket card. |
| **Brainstormer** | brainstorm | Chibi with a floating lightbulb and thought bubbles, messy hair, notebook. |
| **Scribe** | spec-writer, task-planner | Chibi with a long scroll and a fistful of sticky notes, sticking tickets onto the board. |
| **Coder** | coding agent | Chibi robot-kid with a laptop on its head-high backpack, headphones, typing fast, sparks of code. |
| **Tester** | tester agent | Chibi in a lab coat with a magnifying glass and a bug net, a tiny cartoon bug caught in the net. |
| **Three Sceptics** | SkepticScope, SkepticDesign, SkepticRisk | Three identical-sized chibi in matching dark capes, arms crossed, one eyebrow raised, whispering to each other. One holds a ruler, one a blueprint, one a warning triangle. |
| **Compactor** | Bash output compactor | Chibi mechanic feeding a giant tangled paper log through a cute press; a tiny neat cube comes out the other side. |
| **Verifier** | verifier, reviewer | Chibi stamping a big green check on a ticket, wearing round glasses. |
| **Finisher** | finisher | Chibi planting a small flag labeled "ship it" on the DONE column, confetti. |

## Composition

Wide landscape (16:9). The **Kanban board** fills the middle: four columns labeled `TO DO`, `IN PROGRESS`, `TESTING`, `DONE`, with pastel ticket cards. Cards move left to right and get tidier as they go.

- Left third: Brainstormer and Scribe, creating tickets into `TO DO`.
- Center: Orchestrator pointing at one card; Coder at `IN PROGRESS`; Tester at `TESTING` with the bug in the net.
- Right third: Verifier and Finisher at `DONE`, flag and confetti.
- Top: the three Sceptics on a ledge above the board, side-eyeing the plan.
- Bottom corner: Compactor squeezing a huge log into a tiny cube.
- A small wooden sign near the board: `127.0.0.1 home sweet home` (local-only board).
- Slim the token-stack sits on top of the board, waving.

## Style

- Chibi / kawaii anime style, thick soft outlines, flat cel shading with gentle gradients.
- Clean, high-resolution, sticker-like polish, no photo realism.
- Cozy developer-workshop vibe: warm desk lamp light, tiny plants, a mug.

## Palette

Cream background, soft teal and mint for the board, warm gold for the tokens, coral for the Orchestrator accents, lavender for the Sceptics, green only for checks and `DONE`.

## Text on the image (keep it minimal, Gemini garbles long text)

Allowed: `TO DO`, `IN PROGRESS`, `TESTING`, `DONE`, `slimstack`, `ship it`. No other words.

## Avoid

No realistic humans, no dark or grim mood, no brand logos or real product marks, no extra characters beyond the list, no clutter or watermarks, no more than one bug.
